How it works

  1. Send a DM

    Tell her what you want and send photos of your hair in natural light, plus any inspiration pictures you have saved.

  2. Get in touch

    Book a slot yourself, or send a message with photos of your hair in natural light and any inspiration pictures you have saved.

  3. Get your plan and quote

    She will tell you what is realistic on your hair, whether it takes one session or more, and what it costs before you commit.

  4. Lock in the date

    Pick a day that works, and your appointment is set at The Parlour SCV.

  5. Confirm the time

    Vivid work can need longer than a standard slot, so she may adjust the time once she has seen your hair.

  6. Come in

    Show up with your hair down and dry so she can see how it moves and where your color sits.

Services

What you can
book in.

Color, cuts and extensions for people who want something with a bit of edge to it. Everything is custom to your hair, so the exact plan gets set at your consultation.

Color

01
  • Vivids and fashion color Blues, pinks, greens, teal and red. Full head, underlayers, peekaboo panels or face framing.
  • Custom painted panels Hand placed sections and patterns, including the animal print work she is known for.
  • Blonding Lift and tone, from soft bright blonde through to platinum.
  • Highlights and dimensional color Movement through brunette, bronde and copper without going light all over.
  • Gloss and toner refresh Brings faded color back between full appointments.

Cuts

02
  • Layered cut Long layers with movement, one of the things she gets asked for most.
  • Shag and wolf cut Textured, lived in shapes that grow out well.
  • Curtain bangs and face framing Cut to your face shape and how you actually wear your hair.
  • Fringe Blunt or soft, added to an existing cut or as part of a full restyle.
  • Mens cut Clipper and scissor work, finished and styled.
  • Blowout and style Added to any service, or booked on its own.

Extensions

03
  • I-tip extensions, full head Added for length and thickness, then cut in so the join disappears.
  • Extension consultation and color match Booked before your install so the hair is ordered in the right shade.
Pricing is quoted at consultation. Color especially depends on your starting point, your hair length and density, and how far it is going from where it is now. Send a DM with photos and Joon will come back with a price and the time it needs before you commit to anything.

In the chair

Professional lines,
start to finish.

Color is built on Schwarzkopf Professional, including Igora Royal, Igora Vibrance and BlondMe. The loudest vivids come from Danger Jones, and soft pastels from Joico.

Everything gets finished with Kenra Professional styling so you leave with the hair you saw in the inspiration photo.

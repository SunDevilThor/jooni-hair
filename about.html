About

Alternative hair,
done properly.

Joon is an alternative hair stylist working out of The Parlour SCV in Santa Clarita Valley, California.

Her work sits where vivid color meets a real haircut. Blues, pinks, greens and reds, hand painted panels and peekaboos, sitting on shags, wolf cuts and long layers that still look good three months later. She does mens cuts and i-tip extensions too.

Every look is built for the person in the chair. She will tell you what your hair can handle, how many sessions it takes to get there, and what it costs, before anything gets mixed.

I love having such a colorful camera roll. Joon, @jooni.hair
